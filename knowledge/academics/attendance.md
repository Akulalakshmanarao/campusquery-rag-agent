# Attendance Policy

## Purpose
This document contains practice information about student attendance.

## General Information
Students should attend classes regularly and follow the attendance
requirements published by their college.

## Attendance Shortage
Students who have an attendance shortage should contact the academic
office to understand the applicable rules and available procedures.

## Important Note
This is fictional practice content. It is not an official college policy.
Students must consult their official college documents for actual
attendance requirements.
